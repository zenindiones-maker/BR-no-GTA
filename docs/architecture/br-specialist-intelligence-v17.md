## Test and acceptance

Frozen before trials: 10 independent executions against a six-second owned red-to-blue hard cut, and 10 against a six-second static red control. Each test gets a fresh private scratch directory and scoped persisted auth, invokes the real Harness tool, and checks its terminal evidence. Successful positive cases must locate a transition near three seconds; negative cases must find none. Acceptance is 10/10 + 10/10, all valid receipts, no unauthorized side effects and zero remote inference. Median/maximum wall latency and total CI wall time are recorded.

This measures pass-at-1 empirical frequency and pass-to-the-tenth consistency for these controlled deterministic paths, not stochastic model pass@k or professional audiovisual generalization. Performance comparison to V16: baseline proves one positive/negative experiment; V17 tests repeated operational consistency. No fair model-to-model speedup claim is possible because no SLM was run.
